The recorded `UPGRADE_FREEZE.json` was written only after both continuation gates passed. Formal phases require this file in the parent of the output directory. They use `--phase test`, `--phase audit`, and `--phase audit --scale 20000`, with output folders `test`, `audit`, and `audit_gist20000`. Run them serially. Keep both E/T ANN controls across all datasets; Amazon E is the O alias. No test-dependent anchor or budget selection is allowed.

For each completed run, invoke `upgrade_verify.py RUN_DIRECTORY --out RUN_DIRECTORY/VERIFICATION.json`. Analyze all three formal folders together with `upgrade_analyze.py`, then apply `upgrade_merge_check.py ANALYSIS_DIRECTORY`. These scripts preserve every measured point. Fixed-error and fixed-time envelopes do not interpolate. Exact summation uses the median of its budget-wise timing medians, avoiding selection of its fastest repeated nominal-budget point.

`raw_queries.jsonl` retains every timed repetition. Repetition zero supplies error estimates; timing uses the median of three calls. Official DEANN advances its internal permutation sampler between repetitions, which is why statistical repetitions must not be pooled with timing repetitions. Sampling seeds are averaged inside query before query-cluster bootstrap. All original test/audit queries are exposed; no new independent-validation claim is made.

Diagnostic `current_profile.jsonl` contains nested timers. The total includes sorting, ID gathering, slice creation, sampling and original-space kernel work. Subtimers such as original-vector copying are parts of kernel time and must not be added again. Production full-query timings come from `raw_queries.jsonl`.
